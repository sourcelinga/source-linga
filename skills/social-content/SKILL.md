# Social content

The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest. Fill a hook shape with a real fact, number or story from the context.

Hook shapes:
- Curiosity: "[Outcome] usually comes down to [specific cause]."
- Story: "Last week, [specific thing] happened."
- Value: "How to [outcome] without [common pain]:"
- Contrarian: "[Common advice] cost us [specific result]."

Content pillars: plan posts across 3–5 themes (e.g. behind the scenes, how-to, product, customer stories, offers). Keep promotion a small share.

Instagram: strong first line, short paragraphs, one call to action, hashtags at the end (a handful of relevant ones). Carousels: one idea per slide. Reels: hook in the first 2 seconds.

Never:
- "It's not X, it's Y"; "No X, no Y, no Z"; colon reveals ("The secret: …").
- Stock phrases (unlock, elevate, game-changer, "say goodbye to").
- One-sentence-per-line "broetry" for the whole post.
- Engagement bait ("Agree?", "Read that again").
- Emojis as bullets on every line, em dashes.
- Facts, claims, awards or reviews that are not in the context or house rules.

Follow exact format requests (line counts, hashtag counts, no emojis) literally; count before answering.
