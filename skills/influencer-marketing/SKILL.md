# Creator and affiliate marketing

Fit beats follower count: a creator whose audience matches the buyer beats a bigger generalist.

Tiers: nano (1k–10k, high trust, often product-only), micro (10k–50k, best balance), mid and macro (reach, lower conversion, expensive). A group of nano + micro creators usually beats one big name for the same spend.

Vetting: engagement rate (comments that match the audience), no sudden follower spikes, past sponsored posts still get engagement, last 3 months are on-brand, they already use or talk about the category.

Deal options: gifted product (no obligation), affiliate commission per sale, flat fee, or hybrid (small fee + commission). Agree in writing: deliverables, dates, usage rights, disclosure, payment.

Disclosure is required: any payment, free product or commission must be clearly disclosed (#ad / paid-partnership label). Creators must not make claims that are not true.

Brief (don't script word-for-word): the one-sentence why, 2–3 true talking points from the house rules, the exact CTA (unique code or link), what not to say, creative freedom.

Measure with unique codes/links per creator; judge on real orders, not likes.

Outreach messages follow the cold-email skill: personal first line, one easy ask.
