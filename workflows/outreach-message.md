---
name: Outreach message
goal: Get a reply from a business you have never contacted. It must read as personal and honest, not as a template.
fields: Business name, City, Channel (email / WhatsApp / DM), Something specific about them, Your offer
---
Write a short first-contact message. Open with the specific thing about their business. Make one clear offer. End with one easy next step that can be answered in one line. Length: WhatsApp or DM under 70 words; email under 130 words with a subject line. No exclamation-heavy sales tone and no competitor names.
