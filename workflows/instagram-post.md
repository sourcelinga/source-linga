---
name: Instagram post caption
goal: An Instagram caption that earns saves, shares and DMs from the people most likely to buy.
fields: Topic or photo description, Audience, Call to action
---
Write a caption: a hook line, then 2–4 short lines of value (a tip, an origin story or a concrete detail, using only house-rule facts), then the call to action, then 5–8 relevant hashtags. No fake urgency, no review or award claims, no emojis as bullets.
