---
name: Product description
goal: A product description that converts on the website or a marketplace while staying 100% within the known facts.
fields: Product name, Channel (website / marketplace), Notes from the owner, Length (short / long)
---
Write the product description. Structure: a one-line hook, then what it is and what it does for the buyer (only the owner's notes and house-rule facts), then how to use it, then delivery or packing details if known. For a marketplace, add 5 bullet points under 200 characters each. Missing facts: write [ASK OWNER: …].
